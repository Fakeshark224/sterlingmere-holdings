The Challenge

The client executes multi-million dollar HVAC, civil, and heavy engineering projects. Despite their massive scale, their digital presence was running on a cheap, unbranded directory template (like IndiaMART/JustDial).

When pitching to large corporate developers and international builders, their website made them look like a small-scale local shop. They needed a portfolio that matched the actual gravity and budget of the work they perform.

The Solution

We built a heavy-duty, highly professional corporate portfolio designed to command respect from corporate procurement teams.

  • Rugged B2B Design: A strong, high-contrast aesthetic using industrial colors, sharp typography, and full-width construction photography.
  • Project Case Studies: A structured layout to showcase past projects, safety records, and technical specifications to build immediate trust.
  • Secure RFQ Portal: Integrated a Request for Quote (RFQ) system directly on the site, allowing property developers to upload schematics and request bids directly.
