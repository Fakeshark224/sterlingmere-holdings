Luxury Aesthetic

The Challenge

Med Spas often suffer from looking too "sterile" and clinical, or too much like a cheap salon. The client needed a digital architecture that communicated high-end luxury and premium care.

Additionally, their previous method of showing patient results involved static, unaligned photos that looked unprofessional and failed to convince potential patients of the treatment's efficacy.

The Solution

We built a bespoke, dark-mode digital experience that feels like a luxury fashion brand rather than a hospital.

  • Editorial Layouts: Moved away from boring grids to magazine-style, asymmetrical treatment displays.
  • Interactive Engineering: Developed a custom-coded, flawlessly aligned Before/After slider that allows users to seamlessly wipe away wrinkles with their cursor.
  • Brand Positioning: Refined the copy and call-to-actions, replacing cheap phrasing with high-value propositions like "Private Assessment".
