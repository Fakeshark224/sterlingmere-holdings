The Challenge

The Model UN conference had grown exponentially, but their legacy website was built on a fragile shared hosting plan. During the peak registration window, the site crashed completely due to traffic spikes.

Furthermore, international delegates found the site unappealing and difficult to navigate. They needed an enterprise-grade platform capable of handling massive traffic while looking like a top-tier global summit.

The Solution

We completely overhauled their digital infrastructure, transitioning them to a modern, scalable architecture.

  • Ultra-Premium Dark Mode: Implemented a sleek, modern dark theme utilizing glassmorphism and subtle glowing accents to appeal to a younger, tech-savvy international audience.
  • Global CDN Delivery: Deployed the site across a global Content Delivery Network (CDN) to ensure lightning-fast load times for delegates joining from anywhere in the world.
  • Scalable Architecture: Transitioned from a heavy CMS to a highly scalable static framework, ensuring 100% uptime even during massive traffic spikes.
